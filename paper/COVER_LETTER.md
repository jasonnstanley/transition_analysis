# Cover Letter

Editor-in-Chief

International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science and Technology

Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled:

**Transition Analysis Toolkit: A Reproducible Framework for Analysing Student Transition Pathways**

for consideration as an original research article in the *International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science and Technology*.

This manuscript investigates factors associated with successful transition into first-year university mathematics through the application of a transparent and reproducible analytical framework. Using institutional data from a first-stage undergraduate mathematics subject, the study combines statistical analysis, interpretable machine learning, independent verification, and automated reporting to identify the factors most strongly associated with student success.

The principal contribution of the work is the development and demonstration of a reproducible analytical workflow that supports transparent educational data analytics while maintaining consistency between analytical results and published outputs. We believe this contribution aligns well with the journal's interest in research that advances mathematics education through rigorous methodology and practical application.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.

Thank you for considering our submission. We appreciate the time and effort of the editors and reviewers and look forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,

Jason Stanley
